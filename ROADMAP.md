# Roadmap

1. Add a private inquiry intake service with schema validation, reference-based idempotency, staff access control, retention and audit trails.
2. Connect a CRM or transport management system. Add internal administration, transport orders and notifications after the workflow is agreed.
3. Add customer and transport partner accounts, dashboards and tracking with clear permissions and provider-verified events.
4. Introduce distance and price estimates after defining coverage, pricing assumptions and exception handling.
5. Evaluate a transport marketplace, secure document uploads, digital transport documents and online payments after operator and contractual requirements are settled.

## After V2 review

Complete owner configuration, legal sign-off and synthetic acceptance checks before production. Then pilot the private intake inbox and partner verification workflow described in [DZ Transport Manager](docs/DZ-TRANSPORT-MANAGER.md), before quotation and dispatch automation.
