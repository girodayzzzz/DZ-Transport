# DZ Transport Manager

Verify the owner-controlled account’s email and complete the authenticator challenge. The overview counts live inquiry statuses and outstanding partner reviews. Financial totals include completed accepted offers grouped by currency and exclude unpaid implications. Manage invoices and contractual acceptance through the approved business process.

1. Open an inquiry and verify its contact and scope. For a guest inquiry, expand its stored intake details. Add an internal note if the job needs more information.
2. Move it to **Under review**, then **Awaiting carrier offer**. Invite approved carriers individually. Use a coarse pickup and delivery area and the cargo requirements. Leave personal information out of the quote brief.
3. Review carrier quotes in the inquiry. Prepare a customer offer with validity, VAT handling, carrier cost and other expenses. Amounts use minor units. For EUR, enter `125050` for EUR 1,250.50.
4. Move the offer to **Offer sent** once the approved communication step is ready. Account holders can record acceptance in the customer portal. For a guest, obtain verified acceptance through the approved communication process before recording it and the evidence in Manager. These events record operational intent. Complete the separately approved contract before treating the shipment as binding.
5. Assign an approved carrier manually. Include the contact and delivery instructions they need for that shipment. Schedule it, then follow pickup, transit and delivery. A carrier can report those three updates on its own assignment. Complete and archive resolved transports.
6. Verify licences, insurance, company identity and service coverage before approving a partner. Use suspension to revoke access immediately. Review deletion requests and apply approved retention and legal holds.

Use the Manager list’s search and status filters for recent records. An expired offer requires manual resolution. The status workflow allows cancellation but currently has no self-service re-quotation or reassignment flow.
